About

Hi, I'm Marie.

As a child I never stopped drawing, and I was in love with music videos: the way a song could turn into a whole world on screen.

Later I studied in a university art department, where design became my language. Then came photography, and with it the realization that my old dream was within reach: I could make films myself.

Today I bring it all together as a creative producer: drawing, design, photography and film. Before every shoot I learn what you want to feel, and my favorite moment is still when someone recognizes themselves in a new light.

Every photo and film I make has one purpose: celebrating life and picturing joy.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still wondering about something? Write to me, I reply personally.

I can't pose and I never look good in photos. Should I still book?

Absolutely. You don't need any modeling experience: I'll guide you through how to stand, where to look and how to move. There's no need to freeze, because my goal is to capture you as alive as you really are. As we go, I show you the frames, and we adjust anything you'd like to improve. Above all, I want you to enjoy the process and yourself. The session will be easy and full of joy.

What does the editing look like?

Gentle color grading in natural tones with a film feel that will still look beautiful years from now. I don't do heavy retouching or reshape features, only light skin cleanup. The photos show you: your natural beauty and your emotions.

How soon will my photos be ready?

Your full gallery arrives in two to three weeks. A few finished frames come the very next day, so you have something to share right away.

I don't know what to wear.

When we plan the shoot, I help with wardrobe, the overall concept and the color palette. You'll get a PDF guide to choosing outfits from your own closet, and then we refine the look together.

How many photos will I get?

For a one-hour session I guarantee 40 images. Usually there are far more strong frames, and you receive every good one, color-graded.

Where do sessions take place?

City streets, nature, a rented studio, your home or my home studio. The place depends on the idea and the look we're after.

How I work

A few things that help us both.

  1. 01Tell me what kind of session you're dreaming of
  2. 02We talk through the details and settle on a package
  3. 03You choose a date that suits you
  4. 04A 50% deposit holds that day just for you

Your date

I work by appointment only, so each person gets my full attention. Half of the package reserves your day; since I turn other clients away for it, the deposit isn't refundable. The other half is settled after the shoot.

If plans change

I understand, it happens. Give me at least 4 days' notice and we'll simply find a new date. Closer to the session, the deposit stays with me, and the next booking is paid in full ahead of time. And please, if something comes up, just send me a message: I'd always rather hear from you than wait.

Your gallery

Finished images arrive within two to three weeks through a private cloud link. It stays open for 30 days, so there's time to download everything and keep a backup copy.

Sharing the work

By booking with me, you agree that I may show photos from your session in my portfolio, on my website and on social media. If you'd like them kept private, just tell me before the shoot so we can arrange it; a private session adds 50% to the package.
